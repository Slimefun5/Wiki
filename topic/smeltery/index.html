Smeltery & Alloys

Smelt dusts and forge alloys

The Smeltery The Smeltery is a multiblock that smelts dusts into ingots and combines metals into alloys.

Building It It needs a fire source in the block directly below. Light a fire under it, drop your inputs in the dispenser, and right-click to smelt. Check the Guide for the exact Nether-brick build.

The Ignition Chamber The fire can burn out over time. Place an Ignition Chamber holding Flint and Steel next to it to auto- relight and avoid burnout.

What Are Alloys? Alloys mix several metals into stronger ingots, such as: - Duralumin (aluminum mix) - Steel (iron + carbon) - Bronze, Brass, Billon Add the dusts/ingots together in the dispenser to craft them.

Electric Smeltery A powered upgrade that makes alloys only - it will not do plain dust-to-ingot smelting.
