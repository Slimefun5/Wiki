Talismans

Passive luck and protection

Talismans Just keep a talisman in your inventory for a passive effect. You do not hold or equip it.

Warning: many talismans are consumed when they trigger, so carry spares of the ones you rely on. Each has a chance to activate.

Examples - Miner/Farmer/Hunter: double drops - Angel: cancels fall damage - Lava/Fire/Water: emergency resistance - Magician/Wizard: enchant luck - Warrior/Knight: combat boosts

Ender Talismans also work straight from your Ender Chest.

Talismans are items which grant you special effects while in your possession. They can be crafted in a Magic Workbench.

Tier I Talismans only work while you carry them in your inventory, while Tier II (Ender) Talismans can work from inside your Ender Chest.
Note that some Talismans will be consumed upon use.
