Ore Processing

Double your ore yields

Ore Processing These multiblocks let you double your ores and turn junk blocks into resources.

Ore Crusher Crushes ore into 2 dusts, doubling your yield. Put ore in the dispenser and right-click the trigger.

Grind Stone Grinds items into powders (Blaze Rod to Blaze Powder, Bone to Bone Meal, etc.).

Ore Washer Washes Sifted Ore into a random pure ore dust, using a cauldron-based multiblock.

Gold Pan Sift Gravel with a Gold Pan to get flint, clay and nuggets. A Nether Gold Pan sifts Soul Sand for Nether goodies.

Going Electric Later the Electric Ore Grinder crushes ores automatically using power.
