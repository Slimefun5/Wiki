Food & Farming

Juices, jerky and auto-farms

Food & Farming Slimefun adds easy ways to handle hunger early on.

The Juicer turns fruit into juices you can drink (and store in a Cooler).

Jerky from dried meat restores lots of saturation per bite.

Organic Food, made from composted crops, fuels farm machines: - Auto-Breeder auto-breeds nearby animals - Produce Collector gathers milk and other products

Together they keep food and animals running hands-free.

The Food category contains a variety of foods and juices
