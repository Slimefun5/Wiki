Soulbound Items

Keep items when you die

Soulbound Items A Soulbound item stays in your inventory when you die instead of dropping. Perfect for protecting valuable or rare gear.

Binding an item 1. Craft a Rune Anvil and place it. 2. Open it, put the item in one slot and a Soulbound Rune in the other. 3. Take the bound item from the output. The rune is consumed.

Pre-made soulbound tools, armor and backpacks also exist if you prefer to craft them directly.

Tip: bind your best gear before risky trips into the Nether.
