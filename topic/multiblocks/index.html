Multiblock Machines

Build structures to craft

Multiblock Machines Multiblocks are simple machines you build by hand from vanilla blocks in an exact shape. No energy, no GUI.

Most use a dispenser as the trigger block. Hold your input items and right-click the trigger to craft. The Guide shows the exact build layout for each machine.

Common machines - Enhanced Crafting Table - Smeltery - Ore Crusher - Grind Stone - Armor Forge - Magic Workbench

Tip: build at the right height and orientation, or it will not form. Check the Guide layout.
