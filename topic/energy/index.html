Energy Networks

Power your machines

Energy Networks Slimefun machines run on energy, measured in Joules (J). Energy flows through a network that you build.

Starting a network Place an Energy Regulator. It is the heart of the network and shows a floating hologram. Only ONE Regulator per network.

The three roles - Generators produce energy. - Capacitors store surplus. - Machines consume energy.

Connecting it all Components must be within 6 blocks of each other, chained back to the Regulator. Use Energy Connectors to bridge gaps and extend reach.

Reading the network The hologram shows the balance: + surplus (storing energy), or - deficit (not enough power). If supply is short, some machines stall until it refills.

The Multimeter Right-click a machine, capacitor or generator with a Multimeter to read its stored charge.
