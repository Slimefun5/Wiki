Getting Started

Your first steps in Slimefun

Getting Started Welcome to Slimefun! This guide teaches you the basics.

1. Craft the Slimefun Guide Book: place a Book in the center of a crafting grid, surround it with 8 Iron Ingots. 2. Right-click while holding it to open this guide. 3. Browse the categories to see every item and recipe.

Your First Items Items start locked. Click a locked item to research it (see the Research topic).

Build the Enhanced Crafting Table first: it crafts most Slimefun gear. Place a Vanilla Crafting Table with a Dispenser directly below it.

To use it, put the 3x3 recipe into the Dispenser and right-click the table.

Next Steps Set up Ore processing to double your ores, then a Smeltery to make alloys. From there you progress toward electric machines powered by generators and cables.

Take it one machine at a time and have fun!

If you have just started using Slimefun5 it may seem overwhelming or very complicated. You may not know where to start. If that sounds familiar then you have come to the right place!

This guide assumes that you have already installed Slimefun5, otherwise start by reading How to install Slimefun5

1. Slimefun5 Guide

The very first Item you will need on your journey is the Slimefun5 Guide. Do /sf guide to obtain it and Right-Click the Book to open it. You will now be presented with all Categories Slimefun5 can offer. In each Category you will find Items that Slimefun5 (or Addons) have added. You can click on any Item to see it's Recipe. Take a moment to browse through the guide.

2. Researching

You will notice that a lot of Slimefun5 Items could be locked (depending on your Server Setup). This means that you will either need a certain permission to use the item or that you have to research the Item first. To research an Item, simply click on an Item in your Guide that says "LOCKED". Researching Items will cost you XP levels, so make sure to stock up on Experience.

3. Multiblocks

The very first and most basic Machines are found in the Category "Basic Machines", most of these are actually Multiblocks. You can see whether it is a multiblock by hovering over the Item to the left of the recipe. Multiblocks are machines that are built in the world. The 3rd row of the recipe are your bottom blocks, then build above them according to the recipe.

4. Enhanced Crafting Table

This is the first machine you will need to build. You have to place a Crafting Table on top of a Dispenser. Most Slimefun5 Recipes are crafted by putting the recipe into the Dispenser, then right-clicking the Crafting Table. You can read more about this Machine here.

5. Collecting Dust

A lot of Recipes may require various Ingots/Alloys. You start off by crafting a Gold Pan in the Enhanced Crafting Table. Right-Click Gravel with your Gold Pan and you will get Sifted Ore. Then process your Sifted Ore by building an Ore Washer. You will get lots of dusts (e.g. Copper, Tin, Zinc,...) from that, hold onto those and proceed to Step 6.

6. The Smeltery

Now it's time to turn your Dusts into Ingots. Start by building a Smeltery and smelt those into Ingots or Alloys. You can then use these Ingots and Alloys in a ton of recipes.

7. Enjoy the plugin

Now we should have covered everything you need to know in order to start playing with Slimefun5. Have fun and enjoy your adventure!
