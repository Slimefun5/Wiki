Backpacks & Storage

Portable storage on the go

Backpacks & Storage Craft a backpack and right- click to open its portable inventory. Sizes range from 9 slots up to a full 54.

Each backpack is unique to that exact item - its contents travel with it.

The Cooler holds juices and auto-drinks one when you get hungry. Great for long trips.

Soulbound backpacks stay with you when you die.

Warning: never put a backpack inside another backpack - it is blocked to protect your items.

Backpacks are items in which you can store other items.
These can be very useful when looting or scavenging.

These come in 6 tiers, each tier providing more inventory slots to store items in.

Bacpack Tiers

Tier Inventory Slots
Small Bacpack 9
Backpack 18
Large Bacpack 27
Woven Backpack 36
Gilded Backpack 45
Radiant Backpack 54

There is also the Restored backpack, which you can obtain by executing /sf backpack.
This backpack can be used to retrieve lost items from other backpacks, and requires permissions.
