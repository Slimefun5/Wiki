GPS & Teleportation

Waypoints and teleporters

GPS & Teleportation First build and power a GPS Transmitter to put a satellite in orbit. More and higher transmitters raise your network complexity, which speeds up teleporting.

Waypoints Use the GPS Control Panel to manage waypoints, and a Marker Tool to set them. The Emergency Transmitter auto- marks your death spot.

Teleporters Build a Teleporter: a ring of Pylons around a Teleportation Matrix with an activation device. Travel to any waypoint.

Carry a Portable Teleporter to jump to waypoints anywhere.

The GPS category adds various electric and non-electric machines revolving around GPS network.
