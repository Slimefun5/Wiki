Magic & the Altar

Runes, staves and rituals

Magic & the Ancient Altar Build the Ancient Altar: a central Altar block ringed by 8 Ancient Pedestals.

Place a catalyst on each pedestal, hold the central item, and right-click the altar to perform a ritual.

Rituals make Runes (a Blank Rune plus an element) and other magical components.

The Magic Workbench crafts magical items from recipes.

Staves Staves have right-click powers: - Wind Staff: launch yourself - Water Staff: douse fire - Storm Staff: call lightning

This is a category that contains a bunch of items to craft Magical Gadgets with.

Simple Items

Magical Book Cover

This item can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table using Magical Lumps and a book.

Magical Glass

This item can be crafted in a Magic Workbench using Flasks Of Knowledge and Magical Lumps.

Lava Crystal

This item can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table using Magical Lumps, an Ancient Rune [Fire] and blaze powder.

Common Talisman

This item is used as a base to craft Talismans and it can be crafted in the Magic Workbench.

Necrotic Skull

This item is crafted in the Magic Workbench using Magical Lumps and a Wither Skeleton Skull.

Essence of Afterlife

This item is crafted in an Ancient Altar and is used for various high end Magical Gadgets.

Synthetic Shulker Shell

This item can be used a substitute for vanilla Shulker Shells. This way you don't always have to travel in the End to get your Shulker boxes.

Strange Nether Goo

This item is obtained by bartering with piglins and is used to craft the Ancient Rune [Villagers].
