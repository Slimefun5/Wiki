GEO Mining & Oil

Extract oil and resources

GEO Mining & Oil Some resources are hidden in the ground per chunk - mainly Oil and Nether Ice.

Scanning Use a GEO Scanner (or the Portable version) to survey the chunk you stand in. It reports what lies below and how much.

Oil Pump Build and power an Oil Pump in a chunk that contains oil. It slowly extracts Buckets of Oil while it has energy.

Refinery Feed Oil into a Refinery to produce Buckets of Fuel.

Using the fuel Burn Oil or Fuel buckets in a Combustion Reactor for strong power - the empty bucket comes back.

Oil is finite per chunk, so scan widely.
