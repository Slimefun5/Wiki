Hercules' Pickaxe

The Hercules' Pickaxe is an item in the tools category.

Obtaining

The Hercules' Pickaxe can be crafted in a Magic Workbench.

Usage

This tool is similar in function to an Ore Crusher or an Electric Ore Grinder: it crushes iron ore mined with it into iron dust, and gold ore into gold dust.
It also doubles the resources dropped from other ores.
