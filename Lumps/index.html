Lumps

There are two types of Lumps, Ender and Magical. They're both used to craft a lot of Magical Items and Magical Gadgets.

Obtaining

They each come in three tiers, each tier using 4 of the previous to craft.
It is crafted in the Enhanced Crafting Table.
