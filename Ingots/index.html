Ingots

Slimefun5 introduces 25 new ingots, including 17 alloys. They are essential crafting resources.

Metals

The following ingots are obtained by smelting the matching dusts in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory: * Iron Ingot (not added by Slimefun5, but crafted and used in new ways) * Gold Ingot (4 to 24-Carat) * Copper Ingot * Tin Ingot * Silver Ingot * Aluminum Ingot * Lead Ingot * Zinc Ingot * Magnesium Ingot

Alloys

The following ingots are obtained by combining metals in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery: * Aluminum Brass Ingot * Aluminum Bronze Ingot * Billon Ingot * Brass Ingot * Bronze Ingot * Cobalt Ingot * Corinthian Bronze Ingot * Damascus Steel Ingot * Duralumin Ingot * Ferrosilicon * Gilded Iron * Hardened Metal * Nickel Ingot * Redstone Alloy Ingot * Reinforced Alloy Ingot * Solder Ingot * Steel Ingot
