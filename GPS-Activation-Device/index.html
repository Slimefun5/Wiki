GPS Activation Device

The GPS Activation Device is a placeable tool added in the GPS category.

Obtaining

The GPS Activation Device can be crafted in groups of eight in an Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

The GPS Activation Device is a component of a Teleporter.

Variations

The GPS Activation Device has two variations, personal and shared. Personal Activation Device can only be used by the one who placed it, while the Shared Activation Device can be used by anyone who is permitted to activate pressure plates in the area.
