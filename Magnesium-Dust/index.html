Magnesium Dust

Magnesium Dust is one of the nine dusts added in Slimefun5.

Obtaining

Magnesium Dust has a ~11.11% chance of being extracted from Sifted Ore, by processing it through an Ore Washer or an Electric Dust Washer.

Usage

Magnesium Dust can be smelted into Magnesium Ingots in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory.

It is also directly used in creating other items:

  • Magnesium Salt
