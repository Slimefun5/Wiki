Wooden Combine HoeSensible ToolboxAuto-generated

  • Tool
  • A multi-tool hoe that tills, sows and
  • harvests, with a built-in seed bag.
  • Right Click dirt/grass to till a 3x3 area
  • Right Click farmland to sow a 3x3 area
  • Right Click elsewhere to open the seed bag
  • Left Click plants to harvest a 3x3 area
  • Left Click leaves to break a 3x3x3 area
