Buckets of Fuel are [Resources](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Resources) that can be used to fuel certain [Machines](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Electric-Machines).

## Obtaining
Buckets of Fuel can be refined from [Buckets of Oil](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Bucket-of-Oil) using a [Refinery](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Refinery).
