The Synthetic Diamond is a [Resource](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Resources) that can substitute the minecraft diamond in the workbench. It is also used to craft various Slimefun5 items.

## Obtaining
The Synthetic Diamond can be crafted in the [Pressure Chamber](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Pressure-Chamber) using 1 [Carbon Chunk](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Carbon).
