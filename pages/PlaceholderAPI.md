Slimefun5 now also supports [PlaceholderAPI](https://github.com/PlaceholderAPI/PlaceholderAPI).<br>
Check out PlaceholderAPI's Wiki for Info on how to utilise these.

# Placeholders for Researches

## Total Amount of Researches
`%Slimefun5_researches_total_researches%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the total amount of Researches in your Slimefun5 configuration.

## Amount of unlocked Researches
`%Slimefun5_researches_total_researches_unlocked%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the amount of Researches you have unlocked so far.

## Total amount of XP Levels spent
`%Slimefun5_researches_total_xp_levels_spent%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the total amount of xp levels you have spent on Researches.

## Percentage of unlocked Researches
`%Slimefun5_researches_percentage_researches_unlocked%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the percentage of Researches you have unlocked so far.

## Researches Title
`%Slimefun5_researches_title%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with your current Research-Title.

# Miscellaneous Placeholders

## GPS Complexity
`%Slimefun5_gps_complexity%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the complexity of your GPS Network

## Timings / Lag
`%Slimefun5_timings_lag%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the "lag" of Slimefun5.
It shows the time Slimefun5 took to tick all currently active blocks in milliseconds.

## Language
`%Slimefun5_language%`

This Placeholder will be replaced with the player-chosen language for Slimefun5.
