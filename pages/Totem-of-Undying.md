The Totem of Undying is not a Slimefun5 Item, though it has a recipe added by Slimefun5.
It can be found in the [Magical Gadgets](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Magical-Gadgets) category.

## Obtaining
This item can be crafted in the [Ancient Altar](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Ancient-Altar).
