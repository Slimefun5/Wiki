The Scroll of Dimensional Teleposition is part of the [Magical Gadgets](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Magical-Gadgets) category.  

It is used to turn entities around by 180°. 

## Obtaining
This item can be crafted in the [Magic Workbench](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Magic-Workbench).
