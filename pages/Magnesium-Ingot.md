
## Obtaining
Smelting one [Magnesium Dust](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Magnesium-Dust) in a [Smeltery](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Smeltery) or an [Electric Ingot Factory](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Electric-Ingot-Factory) yields one Magnesium Ingot.<br>

Magnesium Ingots can be turned back to [Magnesium Dust](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Magnesium-Dust) by an [Electric Ingot Pulverizer](https://github.com/Slimefun5/Slimefun5/wiki/Electric-Ingot-Pulverizer)

## Usage

Currently Magnesium Ingots are not used directly in any base Slimefun5 recipes.
