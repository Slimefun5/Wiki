Birthday Seasonal Category

The Birthday Seasonal Category is a category which is only open during October (the month of TheBusyBiscuit's birthday). For those who don't know, TheBusyBiscuit is the owner of Slimefun5.
If you ever see it available, take some time to bake it and celebrate his birthday, after all, without him we wouldn't be playing this plugin!

Table of contents
