Gold Ingot

Slimefun5 adds 11 new Gold Ingots to the game.
They are differentiated by their purity (measured in "carats"), with the lowest purity being 4 and the highest being 24.

Obtaining

Smelting Gold Dust in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory produces a 4-carat Gold Ingot.
Higher purity gold is obtained by smelting any carat gold along with a piece of Gold Dust. This "adds" 2 carats, up until 24-carat gold, thus 11 Gold Dust are needed to create a single 24-carat Gold Ingot.

Usage

Slimefun5 Gold Ingots cannot substitute regular Gold Ingots in vanilla recipes. Instead, they are used in new recipes.

Gold Ingot (4-Carat)

4-carat gold can be turned into Gold Dust by crushing it in an Electric Ingot Pulverizer.
It is also used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (6-Carat) * Block Placer

Gold Ingot (6-Carat)

6-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (8-Carat) * Multimeter * Small Backpack

Gold Ingot (8-Carat)

8-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (10-Carat) * Ancient Altar * Ancient Pedestal * Common Talisman

Gold Ingot (10-Carat)

10-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (12-Carat) * Backpack

Gold Ingot (12-Carat)

12-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (14-Carat) * Gold Armor

Gold Ingot (14-Carat)

14-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (16-Carat) * Large Backpack

Gold Ingot (16-Carat)

16-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (18-Carat) * Lava Generator * Woven Backpack

Gold Ingot (18-Carat)

18-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (20-Carat) * Auto-Breeder

Gold Ingot (20-Carat)

20-carat gold is only used to make 22-carat gold.

Gold Ingot (22-Carat)

22-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Gold Ingot (24-Carat) * Gilded Backpack

Gold Ingot (24-Carat)

24-carat gold is used in the following recipes: * Blistering Ingot (33%) * Gilded Iron * Gold Block (24-Carat) * Radiant Backpack * Reinforced Alloy Ingot
