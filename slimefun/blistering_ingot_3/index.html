Blistering IngotSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource
  • ☢ Radiation level: VERY HIGH
  • ⇨ Hazmat Suit required!

Blistering Ingots are Resources that can be used to craft Reactor related items and End-Game Machines.

Obtaining

Blistering Ingots can be crafted in a Heated Pressure Chamber using 1 24-Carat Gold Ingot and 1 Uranium.

Handling

Blistering Ingots are HIGHLY Radioactive. Wearing a Hazmat Suit at all times while handling them is highly recommended.
