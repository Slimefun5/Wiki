GEO MinerSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Mines up resources from the chunk
  • These Resources cannot be mined with a pickaxe
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 48 J/s
  • ! Make sure to Geo-Scan the Chunk first

The GEO Miner is a placeable machine added in the GPS category.

Obtaining

The GEO Miner can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

The GEO Miner requires chunk to be scanned using a GPS Geo-Scanner or a Portable Geo-Scanner.
The GEO Miner consumes 48 J/Sf Tick.

The GEO Miner is used to mine scanned resources, mainly Small Chunk of Uranium, Nether Ice and Salt. This list might be expanded by addons.
