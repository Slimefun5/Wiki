Crop Growth AcceleratorSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Runs on Organic Fertilizer
  • ⇨ Radius: 7x7

  • ⇨ ⚡ 1024 J Buffer

The Crop Growth Accelerator is an Electric Machine which accelerates the growth speed of your crops.

It runs on Organic Fertilizer which is made in the Food Composter.
