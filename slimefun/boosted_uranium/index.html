Boosted UraniumSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource
  • ☢ Radiation level: VERY HIGH
  • ⇨ Hazmat Suit required!

Boosted Uranium is a Resource that can be used to power Nuclear Reactors. Out of all nuclear fuels, Boosted Uranium burns the longest and generates the most energy the fastest.

Obtaining

Boosted Uranium can be crafted through a Heated Pressure Chamber using 1 Plutonium and 1 Uranium.

Handling

Boosted Uranium is VERY HIGHLY Radioactive. Wearing a Hazmat Suit at all times while handling them is highly recommended.
