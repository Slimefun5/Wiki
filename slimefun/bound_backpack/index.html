Soulbound BackpackSlimefunDocumented

  • Backpack
  • A portable storage container that stays
  • in your Inventory even after you die.
  • Size: 36
  • Right Click to open

Soulbound backpack with 36 slots. Stays with you when you die. Your items are never lost.

Soulbound Backpack is a variant on the Woven Backpack.

It only has 36 Inventory Slots which is 18 less than the Gilded Backpack, however, it is kept upon death which is why some people prefer it.
