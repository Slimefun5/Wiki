Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Damascus Steel Ingot:

  • 1 Steel Ingot
  • 1 Iron Dust
  • 1 Carbon
  • 1 Iron Ingot

Total base material cost

  • 4 Iron Dust
  • 16 Coal
