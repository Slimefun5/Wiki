Electric Ore GrinderSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Works as an Ore Crusher and Grind Stone
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 12 J/s

Works as both an Ore Crusher and a Grind Stone, powered by energy. Connect it to an energy network.

The Electric Ore Grinder is an Electric Machine which can crush and grind many items.

It's the electric variant of the Ore Crusher and the Grind Stone, thus it shares the recipes of both machines.
If you want to know all the recipes, just open the machine in the Slimefun5 guide.
