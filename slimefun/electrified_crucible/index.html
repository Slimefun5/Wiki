Electrified CrucibleSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Melts stone-type blocks into a Lava
  • Bucket, or Leaves into a Water Bucket.
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 48 J/s

Melts items into liquids using energy instead of heat.

The Electrified Crucible is an Electric Machine which smelts items into liquid form.

As the name suggests, it is the electrical variant of the Crucible.
If you want to know all the recipes, just open the Crucible's page in the Slimefun5 guide.
