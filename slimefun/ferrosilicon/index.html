FerrosiliconSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Ferrosilicon:

  • 1 Iron Ingot
  • 1 Iron Dust
  • 1 Silicon

Total base material cost

  • 2 Iron Dust
  • 1 Block of Quartz

Usage

In other alloys

  • Redstone Alloy Ingot

Items

  • Photovoltaic Cell
  • Smelter's Pickaxe
  • Pickaxe of Containment
  • Hercules' Pickaxe
  • Explosive Pickaxe
  • Explosive Shovel
  • Pickaxe of the Seeker
