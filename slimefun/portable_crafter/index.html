Portable CrafterSlimefunDocumented

  • Gadget
  • A portable Crafting Table
  • Right Click to open

The Portable Crafter is an Item that has a unique use.

Obtaining

The Portable Crafter can be crafted in the Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

The Portable Crafter can be used while holding the item by right clicking. Upon right clicking, the item will open up a standard crafting table GUI.
