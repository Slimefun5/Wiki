Generates energy by burning plant matter. Fill it with organic fuel and wire it to your network.

The Bio Reactor is a type of Energy Generator.

Obtaining

A Bio Reactor can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

A Bio Reactor must be placed within 7 blocks of an Energy Regulator, an Energy Connector, or an Energy Capacitor in a cross format (not diagonally), and provided with the appropriate fuel.

Tiers

The Bio Reactor only has one tier.
