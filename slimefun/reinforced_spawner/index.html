Reinforced SpawnerSlimefunDocumented

  • Magical
  • A repaired Monster Spawner.
  • Place it down to spawn Mobs.

The Reinforced Spawner is an item in the Magical Gadgets category.
They are the working counterpart of Broken Spawners.

Obtaining

A Reinforced Spawner can be obtained by repairing a Broken Spawner at an Ancient Altar. The mob type will be the same as the original spawner it came from.

Usage

A Reinforced Spawner may be placed down like any other block. It follows the same spawning mechanics as a vanilla spawner.
