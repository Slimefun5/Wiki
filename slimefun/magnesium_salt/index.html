Magnesium SaltSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Magnesium Salt is a Resource that can be used to power a Magnesium-powered Generator.

Obtaining

Magnesium Salt can be crafted in a Heated Pressure Chamber using 1 Magnesium and 1 Salt.
