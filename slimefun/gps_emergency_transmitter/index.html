GPS Emergency TransmitterSlimefunDocumented

  • Gadget
  • Carrying this in your Inventory
  • automatically sets a Waypoint
  • at your Location when you die.

Carry it in your inventory to auto- mark a waypoint where you die. Find your way back to your stuff.

GPS Emergency Transmitter is an item added in the GPS category.

Obtaining

GPS Emergency Transmitter can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table

Usage

While in inventory, GPS Emergency Transmitter creates a waypoint upon player's death with corresponding coordinates.
