Gold DustSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Gold Dust is one of the nine dusts added in Slimefun5.

Obtaining

  • Crushing Raw Gold in an Ore Crusher or an Electric Ore Grinder yields one piece of Gold Dust.
  • Crushing Gold Ore in an Ore Crusher or an Electric Ore Grinder yields two pieces of Gold Dust, effectively doubling it.
  • Gold Dust has a ~11.11% chance of being extracted from Sifted Ore, by processing it through an Ore Washer or an Electric Dust Washer.
  • Crushing a 4-carat Gold Ingot or a normal Gold Ingot in an Electric Ingot Pulverizer produces one piece of Gold Dust.
  • If you are using the Slimefun5OreChunks addon, you can use the same machines to turn Gold Ore Chunks from the GEO Miner into Gold Dust.

Usage

Gold Dust can be smelted into 4-carat to 24-carat Gold Ingots in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory.
It is also used in the Corinthian Bronze Ingot alloy, which can be made in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery.

Gold is one of the most used dusts in Slimefun5 recipes, either as dust or as Gold Ingot.
