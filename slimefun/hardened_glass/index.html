Hardened GlassSlimefunDocumented

  • Component
  • Withstands Explosions

Hardened Glass is a Technical Component that can be used to craft a variety of other Technical Components.

Obtaining

Hardened Glass can be crafted in the Enhanced Crafting Table

Special Properties

Hardened Glass is resistant to most explosions. However, this excludes explosions created by the Wither boss.
