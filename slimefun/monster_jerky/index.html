Monster JerkySlimefunDocumented

  • Food
  • No longer hungry

Made from rotten flesh, but safe to eat. Fills hunger without the poison risk.

Monster Jerky is a Food item added by Slimefun5. When consumed, it removes any Hunger status effect and gives Saturation I for 0.25 seconds.
