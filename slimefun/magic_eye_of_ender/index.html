Magic Eye of EnderSlimefunDocumented

  • Magical
  • Requires full Ender Armor
  • Right Click to shoot an Ender Pearl

The Magic Eye of Ender is part of the Magical Gadgets category.

Together with a full set of Ender Armor, you can shoot enderpearls out of thin air when you right click The Magic Eye of Ender.

Obtaining

This item can be crafted in the Magic Workbench.
