Electric Ingot PulverizerSlimefunDocumented

  • Pulverizes Ingots into Dust
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x

Pulverizes ingots back into dust using energy. Wire it into an energy network to run it.

The Electric Ingot Pulverizer is an Electric Machine which pulverizes Ingots into their corresponding Dusts.

The Electric Ingot Factory is the opposite of this machine.

Electric Ingot Pulverizer Tiers

There is only one tier of the Electric Ingot Pulverizer and it has the following properties:

Speed multiplier: 1.0x
Power Usage: 14 J/Sf Tick
