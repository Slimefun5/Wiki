Stops the Smeltery from consuming its fire. Fill it with Flint and Steel and place it beside the Smeltery dispenser.

The Automatic Ignition Chamber is part of the Basic Machines category.

Usage

The Automatic Ignition Chamber can only be used as an extension on the Smeltery (not the Makeshift Smeltery).
You just put it next to the dispenser of the smeltery and fill it with flint and steel, and it will keep the fire under the smeltery lit up.
