Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Gilded Iron:

  • 1 Gold Ingot (24-Carat)
  • 1 Iron Dust

Total base material cost

  • 11 Gold Dust
  • 1 Iron Dust
