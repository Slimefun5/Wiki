CarbonadoSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource
  • "Black Diamond"

Carbonado is a Resource that can be used to craft a variety of other, high-tier endgame items.
Before you can craft Carbonado, you have to craft Raw Carbonado.

Obtaining

Raw Carbonado can be crafted in the Smeltery using 1 Synthetic Diamond (vanilla minecraft diamonds will not work), 1 Carbon Chunk and 1 Glass Pane.
Carbonado can be crafted in the Pressure Chamber using 1 Raw Carbonado.
