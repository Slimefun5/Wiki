Hold Shift with this in your inventory to attract nearby items. Pulls drops straight to you.

The Infused Magnet is part of the Magical Gadgets category.

It is a useful item that picks up nearby items in a bigger range than you normally could.

Obtaining

This item can be crafted in the Magic Workbench.
