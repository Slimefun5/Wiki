Electric Dust WasherSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Automatically washes Sifted Ore,
  • Pulverized Ore and Sand for their byproducts.
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 6 J/s

The Electric Dust Washer is an Electric Machine which obtains Dusts from Sifted Ore.

It's the electric version of the Ore Washer, only it does not generate by-products and thus improves the yield rate.

Electric Dust Washer Tiers

Tier Power Time Buffer Energy/Ore
I 6 J/Sf Tick 4 s 128 J 24 J
II 10 J/Sf Tick 2 s 128 J 20 J
III 30 J/Sf Tick 0.33 s 512 J 10 J

Tip

Seeing as sifted ore can produce many different dusts, it is recommended to hook it up to a cargo system because it only has 2 output slots and they will fill pretty quickly.
