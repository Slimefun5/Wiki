Zinc DustSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Zinc Dust is one of the nine dusts added in Slimefun5.

Obtaining

Zinc Dust has a ~11.11% chance of being extracted from Sifted Ore, by processing it through an Ore Washer or an Electric Dust Washer.

Usage

Zinc Dust can be smelted into Zinc Ingots in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory.

It is also used in the following alloys for the Smeltery or the Electric Smeltery: * Brass Ingot
