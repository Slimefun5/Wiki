Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Bronze Ingot:

  • 1 Copper Dust
  • 1 Tin Dust
  • 1 Copper Ingot

Total base material cost

  • 2 Copper Dust
  • 1 Tin Dust
