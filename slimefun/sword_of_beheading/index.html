An iron sword with Beheading II. Chance to drop a mobs head on kill. Even higher odds for Wither Skeletons.

The Sword of Beheading is a Weapon added in Slimefun5.
It is an Iron Sword enchanted with Beheading II, which has a chance to drop a Player's or Mob's head upon killing them.

Chance to drop head

The table belows lists the chance for each applicable entity to drop their head upon being killed.

Entity type Drop chance
Player 70%
Creeper 40%
Skeleton 40%
Zombie 40%
Wither Skeleton 25%

If you are using the ExtraHeads addon, it also increases your chance to obtain heads from other mobs.
