Right-click soul sand to collect Nether resources like quartz. The Nether counterpart to the Gold Pan.

The Nether Gold Pan is an item in the Tools category.

How to use the Nether Gold Pan

Right-click a block of Soul Sand while holding a Nether Gold Pan in your main hand.
The block will break and drop Quartz, a Gold Nugget, a Nether Wart, Blaze Powder, Glowstone Dust or a Ghast Tear.

Drop chance

Each item you can get with a Nether Gold Pan has a different probability to drop, listed below.

Item Chance
Quartz 50%
Gold Nugget 25%
Nether Wart 10%
Blaze Powder 8%
Glowstone Dust 5%
Ghast Tear 2%

Automating your Nether Gold Pan

If you prefer to feed your Soul Sand to a machine, check out the Automated Panning Machine.
If you have electricity, you may also be interested in the Electric Gold Pan.
