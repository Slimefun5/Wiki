Hardened MetalSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Hardened Metal:

  • 1 Damascus Steel Ingot
  • 1 Duralumin Ingot
  • 1 Compressed Carbon
  • 1 Aluminum Bronze Ingot

Total base material cost

  • 4 Iron Dust
  • 4 Aluminum Dust
  • 3 Copper Dust
  • 1 Tin Dust
  • 48 Coal

Usage

In other alloys

  • Reinforced Alloy Ingot
  • Redstone Alloy Ingot

Items

  • Hercules' Pickaxe
  • Seismic Axe
  • Coal Generator II
  • Food Fabricator II
  • Food Composter II
