Magical Zombie PillsSlimefunDocumented

  • Magical
  • Right Click a Zombified Villager
  • or a Zombified Piglin to
  • instantly cure it from its curse

Right-click a zombified villager or piglin to instantly cure it. No weakness potion needed.

Magical Zombie Pills are part of the Magical Gadgets category.

They can be used to heal Zombified Villagers into normal Villagers (instantly) or to turn Zombified Piglins into normal Piglins.

Obtaining

Magical Zombie Pills can be crafted in a Magic Workbench.
