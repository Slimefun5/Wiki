Electric SmelterySlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Alloys-Only, doesn't smelt Dust into Ingots
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 20 J/s

Crafts alloys using energy instead of fuel. Alloys only, it will not smelt dust into ingots.

The Electric Smeltery is an Electric Machine which creates Alloys from Ingots.
If you want to smelt Dusts into Ingots, take a look at the Electric Ingot Factory.

It is the automated version of the Smeltery.
