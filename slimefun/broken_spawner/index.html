Broken SpawnerSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource
  • Fractured, must be repaired in an Ancient Altar

Broken Spawners are an item in the Magical Items category.
As the name implies, they do not function in this state.

Obtaining

A Broken Spawner can be acquired by destroying a vanilla spawner with a Pickaxe of Containment. The mob type will be the same as the original, working spawner.

Usage

A Broken Spawner does not work and cannot be placed. However, it can be repaired by performing the correct ritual (found in your Slimefun5 guide) at an Ancient Altar.
