Auto DisenchanterSlimefunDocumented

  • Machine
  • Removes enchantments from an item
  • and stores them in a Book.
  • ⇨ ⚡ Speed: 1.0x
  • ⇨ ⚡ 18 J/s

Removes enchantments from items and stores them on a book, using energy.

The Auto Disenchanter is an Electric Machine can be used to disenchant enchanted tools and weapons.
It will then put these enchantments on a book that you have to provide when you want to disenchant something.

Auto Disenchanter Tiers

There are 2 tiers of the Auto Disenchanter and they have the following properties:

Tier Energy Buffer Speed
I 18 J/Sf Tick 128 J 1.0 x
II 48 J/Sf Tick 1028 J 3.0 x

Tip

Together with the Auto Enchanter you can use this to disenchant Reinforced Armor to obtain Protection IX and Unbreaking IX books and put those on diamond or netherite gear.
