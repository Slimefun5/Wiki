Copper IngotSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Obtaining

Smelting one Copper Dust in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory yields one Copper Ingot.

Copper Ingots can be turned back to Copper Dust by an Electric Ingot Pulverizer

Usage

Copper Ingots are used in the following recipes: * Copper Wire * Battery * Bronze Ingot * Brass Ingot * Electric Dust Washer * Multimeter
