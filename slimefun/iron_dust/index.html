Iron DustSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Iron Dust is one of the nine dusts added in Slimefun5.

Obtaining

  • Crushing Raw Iron in an Ore Crusher or an Electric Ore Grinder yields one piece of Iron Dust.
  • Crushing Iron Ore in an Ore Crusher or an Electric Ore Grinder yields two pieces of Iron Dust, effectively doubling it.
  • If you are using the Slimefun5OreChunks addon, you can use the same machines to turn Iron Ore Chunks from the GEO Miner into Iron Dust.
  • Iron Dust has a ~11.11% chance of being extracted from Sifted Ore, by processing it through an Ore Washer or an Electric Dust Washer.
  • Additionally, crushing an Iron Ingot in an Electric Ingot Pulverizer produces one piece of Iron Dust.

Usage

Iron Dust can be smelted into Iron Ingots in a Smeltery or an Electric Ingot Factory.

It is also used in the following alloys for the Smeltery or the Electric Smeltery: * Steel Ingot * Damascus Steel Ingot * Ferrosilicon * Gilded Iron * Nickel Ingot * Cobalt Ingot

... and directly in creating other items:

  • Magnet

Iron is one of the most used dusts in Slimefun5 recipes, either as dust or as Iron Ingot.
