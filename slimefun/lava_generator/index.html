Lava GeneratorSlimefunDocumented

  • Generator
  • Generates power by burning Lava Buckets.
  • ⇨ ⚡ 512 J Buffer
  • ⇨ ⚡ 20 J/s

Burns lava buckets to generate energy. Fuel it and connect it to an energy network.

The Lava Generator is a type of Energy Generator.

Obtaining

All tiers of lava generators can be crafted in an Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

A lava generator must be placed within 7 blocks of an Energy Regulator, an Energy Connector, or an Energy Capacitor in a cross format (not diagonally), and provided with the appropriate fuel.

Tiers

There are currently two tiers of lava generators.

Tier Energy Buffer
I 20 J/Sf Tick 512 J
II 40 J/Sf Tick 1024 J

Fuel

This generator can only be fueled with lava buckets.

Fuel Burning time Total (Tier I) Total (Tier II)
Lava Bucket 40s 800 J 1.6 kJ
