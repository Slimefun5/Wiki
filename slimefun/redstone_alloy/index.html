Redstone Alloy IngotSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Redstone Alloy Ingot:

  • 1 Redstone Dust
  • 1 Block of Redstone
  • 1 Ferrosilicon
  • 1 Hardened Metal

Total base material cost

  • 10 Redstone Dust
  • 6 Iron Dust
  • 4 Aluminum Dust
  • 3 Copper Dust
  • 1 Tin Dust
  • 1 Block of Quartz
  • 48 Coal

Usage

  • All Energy Capacitors
  • Multimeter
  • GPS Marker Tool
  • Refinery
