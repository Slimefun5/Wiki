Reinforced Alloy IngotSlimefunDocumented

  • Resource

Obtaining

Combining the following items in a Smeltery or an Electric Smeltery yields one Reinforced Alloy Ingot:

  • 1 Damascus Steel Ingot
  • 1 Hardened Metal
  • 1 Corinthian Bronze Ingot
  • 1 Solder Ingot
  • 1 Billon Ingot
  • 1 Gold Ingot (24-Carat)

Total base material cost

  • 8 Iron Dust
  • 4 Aluminum Dust
  • 7 Copper Dust
  • 3 Tin Dust
  • 3 Silver Dust
  • 12 Gold Dust
  • 2 Lead Dust
  • 64 Coal
