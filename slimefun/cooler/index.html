A 27-slot backpack for juices and smoothies. Auto-drinks them when you get hungry.

The Cooler is an Item that has a unique use.
It allows you to carry Juices and automatically consume them when your Player gets hungry.

Obtaining

The Cooler can be crafted in the Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

The Cooler can be used while holding the item by right clicking. Upon right clicking, the item will open up a chest GUI in which the player can place any Juice. If the cooler is in the player's inventory, and the player is hungry, the cooler will automatically feed the player the juices inside.
