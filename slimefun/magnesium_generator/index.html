The Magnesium-powered Generator is an Energy Generator.
It is crafted in the Enhanced Crafting Table.

Usage

A coal generator must be placed within 7 blocks of an Energy Regulator, an Energy Connector, or an Energy Capacitor in a cross format (not diagonally), and provided with the appropriate fuel.

Magnesium-powered Generator Tiers

Currently the Magnesium-powered Generator only has one tier.

Buffer: 128 J Power Production: 36 J/Sf Tick Power per magnesium dust: 720 J
