Coolant Cells

Coolant Cells are a set of Technical Components that are used as coolant for the Nuclear Reactor and Nether Star Reactor.

Obtaining

Reactor Coolant Cell

Reactor Coolant Cells are created in the Freezer.

Nether Ice Coolant Cell

Nether Ice Coolant Cells are created in the Heated Pressure Chamber.

Usage

Reactor Coolant Cell

Reactor Coolant Cells are used to cool the Nuclear Reactor and are required for its operation.

Nether Ice Coolant Cell

Nether Ice Coolant Cells are used to cool the Nether Star Reactor and are required for its operation.
