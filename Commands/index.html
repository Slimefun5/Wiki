Commands

This page contains information about Slimefun5's commands. While there are not a lot of commands in Slimefun5, it is still important to know about these. You can use the prefix /Slimefun5 or /sf. Both will work. But for simplicity, we will list commands as /sf.

() = Required
[] = Optional

Commands for Plugin-Users

Command Description Required Permission
/sf help Gives you a list of all commands in Slimefun5 None
/sf guide Gives you a copy of the Slimefun5 Guide Slimefun5.command.guide
/sf open_guide Opens the Slimefun5 Guide directly Slimefun5.command.open_guide
/sf versions Shows the currently installed Version of Slimefun5 and it's addons Slimefun5.command.versions
/sf stats Shows you your current stats, your title, the amount of total xp levels spent, percentage of completed researches and more. None
/sf search Opens the Guide with a list of items that matched your search term Slimefun5.command.search

Commands for Administrators

Command Description Required Permission
/sf give (Player) (Slimefun5 Item) [Amount] Gives the specified Player the specified Slimefun5 Item Slimefun5.cheat.items
/sf cheat Opens the "Cheat Sheet" - Version of the Slimefun5 Guide, click any Item to give it to yourself Slimefun5.cheat.items
/sf research (Player) (Research / all / reset) Unlocks a certain Research for a Player, unlocks all Researches for a Player or resets all Researches for a Player Slimefun5.cheat.researches
/sf stats (Player) Shows you a specific Player's Stats Slimefun5.stats.others
/sf teleporter (Player) Shows you a specific Player's GPS Waypoints and allows you to teleport to them Slimefun5.command.teleporter
/sf timings Shows you Slimefun5's Diagnostics Tools, use it to identify the impact on your server from certain machines Slimefun5.command.timings
/sf debug_fish Gives you a debugging tool. Use it to query or remove Slimefun5 Block Data Slimefun5.debugging
/sf backpack (Player) (ID) Gives you a restored backpack matching the parameters. Should only be used to retrieve the items Slimefun5.command.backpack
/sf charge Fully charges the item in your hand. Slimefun5.command.charge
